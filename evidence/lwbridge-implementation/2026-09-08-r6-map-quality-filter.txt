Exact selector recovery
- quality field raw offset 0x00C89E59; xref 0x140273E02.
- n: selector compare at 0x140273F91; ecx=1 at 0x140274027; shared helper call 0x14027402C.
- r: selector compare at 0x140273F9A; ecx=2 at 0x140273FAD; shared helper call 0x14027402C.
- sr: selector compare at 0x140273E5E; ecx=3 at 0x140273E73; shared helper call 0x14027402C.
- ssr: selector compare at 0x140273F61-0x140273F74; ecx=4 at 0x140273F84; shared helper call 0x14027402C.
- ur: selector compare at 0x140273E53; literal SQL assembled at 0x140273FCD/0x140273FDA as `quality >= 5`.

Shared equality helper
- Function: 0x14027A129-0x14027A1E3.
- Reads the `quality` field marker at 0x14027A13B.
- Uses the ` = ?` format descriptor at raw file offset 0x00C8B3F7.
- Stores a bound integer parameter from rdi at 0x14027A1CD.
- Therefore the ordinary selector predicates are exactly:
  n -> quality = ? with 1
  r -> quality = ? with 2
  sr -> quality = ? with 3
  ssr -> quality = ? with 4
  ur -> quality >= 5 with no quality parameter

Truck UR special-quality exclusion
- After the ordinary quality branch, control flow at 0x140274033-0x140274078 checks kind `truck` and selector `ur`.
- Only that branch references raw predicate 0x00C89E60 at xref 0x140274093:
  COALESCE(CAST(json_extract(data_json,'$.isSpecialURQuality') AS INTEGER),0) = 0
- Thus ordinary truck UR excludes special-UR/reindeer rows. Railway/dispatch/ghost UR do not receive this extra guard from this branch.

Refinement of LWB-R6-012
- LWB-R6-012 correctly recorded the byte adjacency between `quality` and the special-UR exclusion predicate, but its broad interpretation that ordinary quality generally excludes special-UR rows was incomplete.
- LWB-R6-013 follows the executable control flow and narrows the predicate to kind=truck plus selector=ur. Active documentation should use this narrower result while preserving R6-012 as historical evidence.

Validation and limits
- RECOVERED static and deterministic verifier-backed; not LIVE-PROVEN.
- The backend also accepts a numeric quality representation and binds equality, but the recovered frontend emits the five string selectors above. Production reconstruction should admit those recovered frontend forms and keep other forms fail-closed unless separately required/proven.
- Completion-status clocks, plunderability, treasure visibility/lucky behavior, remaining sorts, public map_data_options assembly and authoritative map_summary selection remain separate DB-05 gaps.
